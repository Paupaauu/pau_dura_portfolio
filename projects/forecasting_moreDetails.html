Historical Data Analysis & Forecasting

Assessment of historical data quality, identification of behavioural patterns and construction of fine-tuned forecasts, all organised into visual, interactive dashboards that make the results easy to interpret and support decision-making.

// how the work is carried out, step by step

1. Data collection and consolidation()

Integration of the client's source data (CSV, Excel or BigQuery), even when it comes from different systems, into a single consistent series per channel or segment and per time interval.

2. Data quality assessment()

Automatic review of the history to detect days with no data, public holidays, service hours, residual or discontinued segments and atypical days (incidents, one-off campaigns). Every data-cleaning decision is agreed with the client and documented.

3. Analysis of historical behaviour()

Identification of the trend, annual seasonality, the weight of each day of the week, the time-of-month effect and the intraday distribution in 30-minute slots, by channel or segment.

4. Building the forecast()

The daily forecast for each channel or segment is built by comparing three methods: the average of the last 4 weeks, seasonal decomposition (day of the week, time of the month, trend and annual seasonality) and Holt-Winters exponential smoothing (ETS). Each method is validated against the history itself (backtesting) and the one with the lowest error (WAPE) is chosen, with a P10–P90 confidence band. The forecast includes the effect of public holidays and, if the client provides target volumes, is adjusted to them without losing the patterns. Finally, it is split into 30-minute slots and aggregated to monthly level, so that all granularities add up.

5. Interactive dashboard()

Results presented in a dashboard that brings together the history, patterns, forecast, data quality and methodology, with findings written in plain language for the client.

6. Export for planning()

Download of the forecast at 30-minute, daily or monthly level, ready for Excel or BigQuery and for workforce sizing.

7. Updates and tracking()

Monthly update with new data and comparison of forecast against actuals, to measure the deviation and fine-tune the model.
